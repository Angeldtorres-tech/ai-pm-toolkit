**Author:** Angel Torres | **Date:** 2026-02-24

---

## 1. Launch Strategy

### Internal Product Launch (Phase 1-2)
The CIE launches internally at Marigold before any external consideration.

**Launch Approach: Land and Expand**
1. **Land:** One PM on one product line (Sailthru) for 4 weeks
2. **Validate:** PM confirms brief is valuable; accuracy metrics meet targets
3. **Expand:** Roll to remaining product lines (Cheetah, Selligent)
4. **Scale:** Add executive dashboard; integrate with roadmap tools

### Why Internal First
- De-risks the product with a captive, motivated user (internal PM team)
- Generates real accuracy and impact data before any external claims
- Builds internal case study for potential productization

---

## 2. Internal Rollout Plan

### Week 1-2: Design Partner Phase
- **Who:** 1 PM (Sailthru product line)
- **What:** Manual brief with AI-assisted analysis
- **Goal:** Validate classification taxonomy, brief format, delivery cadence
- **Activities:**
  - Daily 15-min check-in with design partner PM
  - Iterate on classification categories based on real feedback
  - Refine brief template based on PM preferences
  - Establish eval baseline (first 100-item audit)

### Week 3-4: MVP Launch
- **Who:** Same PM + PM manager awareness
- **What:** Automated pipeline with first real brief delivery
- **Goal:** Deliver first automated brief; collect actionability feedback
- **Activities:**
  - First automated weekly brief delivered Monday 7 AM
  - PM rates each section (1-5 actionability score)
  - Fix top 3 issues identified in first brief
  - Second brief demonstrates improvement

### Week 5-8: Team Expansion
- **Who:** All PMs across 3 product lines
- **What:** Full automated briefs + alerting for early adopters
- **Goal:** Multi-PM adoption; cross-product intelligence begins
- **Activities:**
  - Onboarding session for PMs (30 min): what CIE is, what the brief means, how to drill down
  - Each PM configures their alert thresholds
  - Bi-weekly feedback session with PM team
  - Cross-product theme identification begins

### Week 9-12: Leadership Adoption
- **Who:** VP Product + Product Leadership Team
- **What:** Executive dashboard + quarterly impact report
- **Goal:** Leadership uses CIE data in strategic planning
- **Activities:**
  - Executive dashboard walkthrough with VP Product
  - First quarterly impact report: themes influenced roadmap, accuracy metrics, time savings
  - Integration with roadmap tool (Linear/Productboard)
  - First board-ready customer sentiment summary

## 3. Adoption Metrics

### Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4): Design Partner
| Metric | Target | How We Measure |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| PM engagement | 5+ interactions/week with system | Login tracking + feedback sessions |
| Brief usefulness | PM says "I would miss this if it went away" | Qualitative interview |
| Classification quality | PM agrees with >80% of classifications | Manual audit |

### Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8): Team Rollout
| Metric | Target | How We Measure |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Adoption rate | >80% of PMs read weekly brief | Email open tracking |
| Brief actionability | Average >3.5/5 rating | Post-brief survey (1 question) |
| Time savings | Self-reported reduction in manual review | Monthly PM survey |
| First influenced decision | At least 1 roadmap item cites CIE data | PM self-report |

### Phase 3 (Weeks 9-12): Leadership
| Metric | Target | How We Measure |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Executive dashboard usage | VP Product logs in 2+/month | Analytics |
| Board reporting | CIE data included in quarterly review | Presentation review |
| Cross-product insights | 3+ themes identified across product lines | Brief analysis |

## 5. Potential Productization Path (Phase 4+)

### The Opportunity
Marigold serves 40,000+ marketing teams. These teams face the same customer intelligence challenge. CIE could become a feature of the MAI platform.

### Productization Requirements (NOT Phase 1-3)
- Multi-tenant architecture
- Self-service source configuration
- Customer-facing documentation
- SLA and support model
- Pricing and packaging

### Revenue Potential
- 40K customers x 5% adoption x $200/month = $4.8M ARR potential
- Differentiator against Braze, Klaviyo, Iterable (none offer embedded customer intelligence)

**Note:** Productization is a Phase 4+ consideration. Internal validation must come first. Including this to show strategic thinking, not to commit resources prematurely.

## 6. Risks to Adoption

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|------------|
| PMs don't read the brief | Medium | Co-design format; make it scannable; track open rates; iterate |
| PMs don't trust AI classifications | Medium | Show confidence scores; link to source; publish accuracy metrics |
| Too many alerts cause fatigue | Medium | Conservative default thresholds; PM controls their own alerts |
| Leadership doesn't see strategic value | Low | Quarterly impact report with specific examples of influenced decisions |
| Engineering resources pulled for other priorities | Medium | MVP requires only 4 weeks of 1 engineer; modular design means pausing doesn't lose progress |
